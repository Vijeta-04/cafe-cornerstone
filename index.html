✦ Our Story

Crafted with
Heart & Soul

Cafe CornerStone by Pallavi Daga is a women-owned premium café nestled in the heart of Jaipur's prestigious C-Scheme neighbourhood. Born from a passion for exceptional experiences, every detail — from the hand-selected coffee beans to the artfully plated desserts — reflects an unwavering commitment to quality.

Known for exceptional hospitality, gourmet vegetarian cuisine, handcrafted beverages, and a sophisticated ambience that feels both luxurious and warmly welcoming, Cafe CornerStone has become Jaipur's favourite gathering place for discerning guests.

Whether you're here for a quiet morning espresso, a celebratory brunch, or an intimate family dinner — we promise every moment with us will be memorable.
